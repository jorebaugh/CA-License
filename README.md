# CA License Study

Offline-capable study app for the USCG OUPV Near Coastal captain's license (ONC07).

**Tabs**
- **Study:** quiz, flashcards and answers-only review of 636 cards (MLS Rules of the Road, Deck General, Nav General + official USCG samples), with a trouble pile.
- **Exam:** practice exams that match the MLS final — Rules of the Road 50 Q / 90%, Deck General 50 Q / 70%, Navigation General 50 Q / 70%. Full, half or quick length, timer, flagging, score report and miss review.
- **Drill:** endless generated piloting problems with worked answers: TVMDC both ways, deviation on a range, leeway, variation by year, light range, 60D = ST, distance abeam.
- **Guides:** all four study guides, readable offline.

**Study guides (PDF):** `Rules_of_the_Road_Study_Guide.pdf`, `Deck_General_Safety_Study_Guide.pdf`, `Navigation_General_Study_Guide.pdf`, `Chart_Plotting_Study_Guide.pdf` (page images for the in-app viewer are in `guides/`).

**iPad:** open the GitHub Pages site in Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen** → open it on Wi-Fi and wait for the Guides tab to show "Ready offline". After an update, open the app twice while online.
